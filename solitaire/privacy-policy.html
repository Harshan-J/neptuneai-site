Privacy Policy

Solitaire

Effective 17 Oct 2026  ·  Package com.harshanaj.solitaire

In plain terms: Solitaire keeps every deal, every save, and your settings entirely on your device - there's no account and nothing you do in the app is ever sent to us. The only data leaving your device is what our advertising partners (Google, Unity and Meta) collect to show ads and, if you choose to subscribe to the optional Solitaire Club, the purchase itself, which Google Play handles. Both are described in full below.

01 Information we collect

Solitaire does not require an account or sign-in of any kind, and we do not collect, transmit, or have access to anything about how you play - your deals, moves, undo history, stars, or stats. Every game of Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, or Pyramid is played, scored, and saved entirely on your device.

We do not operate any server that Solitaire sends data to. There is no network request in any feature of this app other than the ones our advertising partners make to show ads and the ones Google Play makes to process an optional subscription, described next.

02 Third-party services

Solitaire is free to use and supported by ads from our advertising partners, Google, Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms), whose ads reach the app through Google's ad mediation. The ads include banner, full-screen (shown between deals and when the app returns to the foreground), native (at the end of menus and in the win screen) and rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for a hint - a hint is always given once the ad finishes, whether or not you watch it through. To show ads, their SDKs may collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • General device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, Unity and Meta, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, Unity's Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Solitaire Club (optional subscription). Solitaire Club is an optional paid subscription that removes ads and unlocks extra table felts and card backs, hints without watching an ad, replaying past daily deals and the full game history. Payment is taken and managed entirely by Google Play through the Google Play Billing library: we never see or store your card or other payment details, your name or your Google account. The app only learns whether your subscription is active (a purchase token and its status) so it can unlock the features, and keeps a copy of that status on your device so it also works offline. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; you can manage or cancel one at any time in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions). While Solitaire Club is active no ads are shown, so the advertising partners above collect nothing from the app. Google processes purchase data under Google's Privacy Policy.

In the European Economic Area and UK, Solitaire uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before any personalized advertising data is collected, in line with GDPR requirements.

03 Permissions we use

Solitaire does not ask you to grant any permission - no storage, camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications access. It declares only the standard permissions Android grants automatically at install: vibration (for the optional haptic feedback you can switch off in Settings), and the internet/network-state and Google Play billing permissions that the advertising and subscription libraries above need to work online.

04 Data storage & retention

Solitaire saves your in-progress deal (so closing the app mid-game resumes where you left off), your level progress and stars for Klondike, FreeCell, Spider, and Pyramid, your win/streak stats and a short history of your most recent games (result, time and score), the coins and level you earn, which daily deals you have solved and your daily streak, your table, card-back and card-face choices, whether Solitaire Club is active, and your settings (like draw-1 vs draw-3, sound, haptics and left-handed layout). All of this is stored only in the app's local storage on your device - it is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

Uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is currently no in-app option to clear individual pieces of this data short of uninstalling, since none of it is sensitive (it's entirely game progress).

05 Children's privacy

Solitaire is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

06 Your choices

  • Ad personalization — manage or reset your consent choice at any time from your device's Google Ads Settings, or opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Local data — uninstall the app to remove everything described above.

07 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above. Continued use of Solitaire after a change means you accept the updated policy. We recommend checking back periodically.
