Privacy Policy

Compressor: Image & PDF

Effective 20 Oct 2026  ·  Android package and iOS bundle ID com.harshanaj.compressor

In plain terms: Compressor processes your photos and PDFs entirely on your device. Files you compress, convert, edit, merge, split, scan, or sign are never uploaded anywhere. On Android, two things leave your device: data our advertising partners (Google, Unity and Meta) use to show ads, and basic performance metrics from Google's on-device ML tools (never your photos). On iPhone and iPad nothing about you or your files leaves your device: the app has no ads and no tracking. Details below.

This policy covers Compressor on Android and on iPhone and iPad. Where the two differ, the section says so. On iPhone and iPad the app shows no ads and does not track you, the on-device tools are Apple's, and Apple takes the payment for the subscription the app requires, so the app only learns whether your trial or subscription is active.

01 Information we collect

Compressor does not require an account, and we do not collect, transmit, or have access to the photos, PDFs, or other files you process in the app. All compression, conversion, photo editing, merging, splitting, scanning, and signing happens locally on your device using your device's own processing power. On Android, document scanning uses Google's on-device document scanner (part of Google Play services), which processes the camera image on your phone. On Android, Compressor itself does not request camera access, and the signature you draw to sign a PDF is stored only in the app's private storage on your device.

Edit Image also works entirely on your phone. On Android, the background tools use Google's on-device subject detection (ML Kit), and the AI eraser runs a model that is downloaded once from Google Play the first time you use it. Your photos are never sent to Google or to us.

iPhone and iPad. The same applies on iOS. Document scanning uses Apple's on-device VisionKit scanner, and the app asks for camera access only so that scanner can work; the camera image is processed on your device and goes nowhere else. Edit Image uses Apple's Vision framework to find the subject of a photo and an eraser model that is built into the app, so nothing is downloaded and your photos never leave your device. The signature you draw to sign a PDF is stored only in the app's private storage.

When Keep photo metadata is on (the default), a compressed or converted photo keeps the original's details, such as camera, capture time and location (GPS). This is copied on your device. Anyone you share the photo with can see these details, as with the original photo. Turn the option off in Convert & Compress or in Settings to leave them out.

We do not operate any server that Compressor sends your files to. There is no upload step in any feature of this app.

02 Third-party services

On Android, Compressor is free to use and supported by ads. On iPhone and iPad it is a subscription app with no ads (described below). On Android the ads come from our advertising partners, Google, Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) and Meta Audience Network (Meta Platforms), whose ads reach the app through Google's ad mediation. The ads include banner, full-screen, native (in the History list, at the end of the menus and under finished results) and optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch to process more files at once. To show ads, their SDKs may collect and process data such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • General device information (model, OS version, language, screen size)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address, not GPS)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, Unity and Meta, not by us, and is governed by Google's Privacy Policy Unity's Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's business data responsibility site.

iPhone and iPad: no advertising. The iPhone and iPad app contains no ads and does not use your advertising identifier, so it does not ask for tracking permission or an ad-consent choice, and no advertising data is collected.

Compressor subscription (iPhone and iPad). Using the iPhone and iPad app requires a subscription, weekly or monthly, that starts with a 3-day free trial for new subscribers. Until a trial or subscription is running, the app shows only the subscription page. You buy it through your Apple ID, and Apple takes the payment, so we never see your payment details, name or Apple ID. The app only learns whether your subscription is active (through Apple's StoreKit) and keeps the date it runs to on your device so the app also opens offline. The subscription renews automatically at the end of each period unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before; manage or cancel it in your Apple ID account settings. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy; the Terms of Use are Apple's standard licensed application end user license agreement.

Google ML Kit (Android only; part of Google Play services) powers the document scanner and the subject detection in Edit Image. It processes images on your device and never sends them anywhere, but it may update its models and send Google basic performance metrics, such as device model and processing time. See Google's ML Kit terms.

On Android in the European Economic Area and UK, Compressor uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent before any personalized advertising data is collected, in line with GDPR requirements.

03 Permissions we use

  • File access — on Android, requested per file, per action, through Android's Storage Access Framework. On iPhone and iPad you choose files and folders with the system Files and Photos pickers, and the app only gets what you pick. You choose exactly which file or folder to share with the app; we don't get standing access to your storage. Files you send to Compressor from another app (Share or Open with) are copied to the app's private temporary folder for processing and removed when you clear the cache.
  • Notifications — used only to show the progress and completion status of batch compression jobs running in the background.
  • Photo location — requested only when you compress photos with Keep photo metadata on. Android removes the location from photos it gives to an app without this permission, so it is needed to keep the location in the compressed copy. Android shows the request as access to your photos and videos; Compressor still only opens the files you choose. The location is read and written on your device and never sent to us or anyone else. If you decline, the app works as before and compressed photos don't keep their location.
  • Photos (iPhone and iPad) — to save results to your photo library the app asks to add photos. If you choose to save into a Photos album it asks for access to your library so it can list your albums and add to the one you pick. It does not upload or share your photos, and if you decline you can save to a folder in Files instead.
  • Camera (iPhone and iPad) — used only by the document scanner, on your device.

04 Data storage & retention

Compressor keeps a local history of your past compression jobs (file names, sizes, and timestamps) so you can find results later. This history is stored only in the app's local database on your device — it is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

You can clear this history, and the app's temporary working files, at any time from Settings → Data. Uninstalling the app removes all of it.

05 Children's privacy

Compressor is a general-audience utility app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

06 Your choices

  • Ad personalization (Android) — manage or reset your consent choice at any time from your device's Google Ads Settings, or opt out of personalized ads at adssettings.google.com.
  • Subscription (iPhone and iPad) — manage or cancel in your Apple ID account settings (Settings → your name → Subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • Local data — clear compression history and cache from Settings inside the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
